How to Play

There are 4 stages to complete.

In each stage, you need to click on the AI generated images. At the end of all 4 stages you will reveal a code.

You only have 10 seconds per stage! So use your time wisely!

Rule #1: when you click on a real image, you will be sent back to the previous stage.

Rule #2: if you fail to complete a stage within the time limit, you will also be sent back to the previous stage.

Good luck!
